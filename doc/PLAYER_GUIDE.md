# Alphestival

Welcome to the Alphestival! To celebrate the end of the alpha, you can play various minigames that will allow you to earn various in-game rewards.

You can play to earn virtual coins :coin: by playing Crossfire Arena or in minigames, and spend them in #shop, in #roulette or in #community-pool.

All bot commands work only in the **Alphestival** category.


---

## General (any Alphestival channel)

**commands:**


/join
Generate your in-game player ID. Use it in the game to link your account in Crossfire Arena (Settings -> Account -> Discord ID).

/balance
Show your coin balance.

/leaderboard
Show top coin holders.

/help
Show the full command list for your role.

---

## daily-questions

Answer a daily question about the game to earn some coins. A new poll is posted automatically each day — vote on the message’s poll. When the question closes, everyone who voted for the correct answer receives coins.

*(No commands — just vote on the poll in the message posted in this channel.)*

---

## community-pool

Heads or tails game: bet coins, double on heads or donate to the community pool on tails. The more you risk, the bigger the pot — and the pool grows to unlock reward tiers for everyone.

**commands:**

/toss coins:
Starting coins for the toss streak. Your pot doubles on heads (then choose to donate to the pool or toss again); on tails your initial amount is donated to the pool.

---

## roulette

When a round is open, bet coins for a chance to win the round’s reward. One winner is drawn; your chance to win is proportional to your share of the total pool (e.g. 30 coins in a 100-coin pool = 30% chance).

**commands:**

/roulette_bet amount:
Place a bet in the current roulette round.

/roulette_status
Show current round, total pool and who bet how much.

---

## shop

Spend your coins on in-game rewards. Admins post a message with rewards and **Buy** buttons — click to purchase (one per reward type, depending on config).

*(No commands — use the Buy buttons on the message posted in this channel.)*

---

## daily-race

Leaderboard based on in-game daily collects. Link your Discord account to the game first, then your score appears here.

**commands:**

/daily_score
Show your daily race score, rank, streaks and total collects.
